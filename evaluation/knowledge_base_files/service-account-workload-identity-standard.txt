Service Account and Workload Identity Standard
Document owner: Identity Engineering
Review frequency: Annual
Classification: Internal

Purpose
Constrain non-human identities to a documented service purpose and lifecycle.

Scope
Applies to backend services, scheduled jobs, ingestion workers, and deployment automation.

Definitions
Control owner: the role accountable for implementation and evidence.
Exception: a documented, approved, and time-bounded deviation.

Roles and responsibilities
Service owners request workload access.
Identity Engineering provisions identities.
Operators remove unused credentials.

Mandatory rules
Use a dedicated identity per service where feasible.
Grant minimum database and vector operations.
Do not reuse an employee token as a service credential.
Rotate credentials and certificates on schedule or compromise.
Separate development, test, and production identities.

Operating procedure
Record owner, environment, dependencies, and expiry.
Approve scopes before provisioning.
Store credentials in protected runtime configuration.
Monitor service use and privilege changes.
Disable and remove identities when the workload retires.

Examples
A RAGAS runner uses evaluation credentials and does not change the production LLM.
A deployment role cannot grant user document permissions.

Exceptions
Temporary broad scope requires explicit approval and expiry.
A missing owner is grounds to disable or reassign the identity.

Audit evidence and review
Review service identity inventory quarterly.
Audit provisioning and decommission evidence.
