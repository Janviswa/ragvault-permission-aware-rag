Backup and Restore Standard
Document owner: Infrastructure Reliability
Review frequency: Annual
Classification: Internal

Purpose
Ensure recoverable data copies are protected and restoration is verifiable.

Scope
Applies to relational records, uploaded sources, configuration, and derived vector indexes.

Definitions
Control owner: the role accountable for implementation and evidence.
Exception: a documented, approved, and time-bounded deviation.

Roles and responsibilities
Data owners classify backup content.
Operators run and test backups.
Security reviews restore exposure.

Mandatory rules
Restrict backup access independently from production access.
Protect backup transfer and storage.
Define retention and deletion behavior.
Do not claim immediate erasure from immutable backup media.
Restore derived indexes only with current ACL state.

Operating procedure
Identify source of truth and recovery point.
Restore into a controlled environment.
Reapply tenant and document authorization metadata.
Validate counts, integrity, and access boundaries.
Record acceptance before production use.

Examples
A Qdrant rebuild may regenerate vectors from authorized source documents.
A retained backup expires under its documented lifecycle.

Exceptions
A legal hold changes disposal only within the recorded scope.
Emergency restore is monitored and reviewed.

Audit evidence and review
Record restore date, operator, source, and test results.
Exercise recovery at the approved frequency.
