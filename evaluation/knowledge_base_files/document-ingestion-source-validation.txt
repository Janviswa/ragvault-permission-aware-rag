Document Ingestion and Source Validation Standard
Document owner: Content Platform Engineering
Review frequency: Annual
Classification: Internal

Purpose
Define safe intake and validation for business documents entering a searchable knowledge collection.

Scope
Applies to TXT, PDF, and DOCX uploads and their extracted text, chunks, embeddings, and source metadata.

Definitions
Control owner: the role accountable for implementation and evidence.
Exception: a documented, approved, and time-bounded deviation.

Roles and responsibilities
The uploader confirms ownership and permitted use.
The document owner approves audience and classification.
Platform Engineering maintains parsers and ingestion evidence.

Mandatory rules
Supported extensions are checked before parsing.
Malformed or empty extraction is rejected instead of indexed.
The stored source remains associated with its tenant and document identity.
Extracted content is treated as untrusted data.
Ingestion never grants access that the source permission record does not grant.

Operating procedure
Record uploader, tenant, source name, and classification.
Extract text using the format-specific loader and inspect extraction errors.
Chunk content with the configured overlap and generate normalized embeddings.
Attach document and ACL metadata to each vector point.
Verify source count and vector count after ingestion or replacement.

Examples
A scanned image with no extractable text requires an approved OCR path.
A DOCX with tables is checked for extraction limitations before operational use.

Exceptions
A parser exception blocks indexing and is escalated with the source identifier.
A retry is recorded and does not create duplicate points for the same chunk.

Audit evidence and review
Retain extraction outcome, parser version, and ingestion timestamp.
Review malformed-file and duplicate-source rates quarterly.
