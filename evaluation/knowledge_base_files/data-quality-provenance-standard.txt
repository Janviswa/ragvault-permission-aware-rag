Data Quality and Provenance Standard
Document owner: Knowledge Operations
Review frequency: Annual
Classification: Internal

Purpose
Ensure searchable content is attributable, current, and suitable for its intended audience.

Scope
Applies to uploaded documents, extracted text, generated evaluation corpus, and derived chunks.

Definitions
Control owner: the role accountable for implementation and evidence.
Exception: a documented, approved, and time-bounded deviation.

Roles and responsibilities
Content owners confirm source accuracy.
Knowledge operators maintain metadata.
Reviewers assess stale or conflicting sources.

Mandatory rules
Every indexed source has an owner and unique identity.
Preserve source provenance during extraction and chunking.
Do not represent illustrative policy as adopted company policy.
Resolve conflicting versions before broad indexing.
Remove duplicates without dropping permission history.

Operating procedure
Check source title, date, audience, and classification.
Compare extracted text with the original where format risks exist.
Record superseded status and replacement reference.
Review duplicate titles and filenames.
Re-ingest through the authorized workflow after approval.

Examples
A PDF extraction is checked for missing pages.
A revised policy is not presented beside a stale copy without a version label.

Exceptions
Unverified content remains quarantined from production retrieval.
A source exception has an owner and expiry.

Audit evidence and review
Retain source checksum or version evidence where supported.
Review stale-source reports quarterly.
